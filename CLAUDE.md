# CLAUDE.md — engineering

This repository holds the cross-project engineering principles and standards. Read CHARTER.md first.

- Principles and standards are append-only. Refine them or supersede them. Never delete or weaken a rule.
- Every change to PRINCIPLES.md or standards/ needs a new ENG-DEC entry in DECISIONS.md.
- Do not mark anything `conformant` in CONFORMANCE.md without a cited test, commit, or file.
- Do not claim ISO/IEC certification or compliance. These standards are reference models only (ENG-DEC-004).
- Commit format: `type(scope): description — ENG-…`. No AI co-author lines. Only the human pushes and merges.
